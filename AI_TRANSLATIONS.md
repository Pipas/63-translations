# AI-generated Spanish translations

The `es-ES` and `es-419` locale files in this repository are **AI-generated drafts**.
They have not yet been reviewed by a native Spanish translator and should not be treated
as final copy until that review is complete.

## Scope and method

- `es-ES` was translated directly from the `en-GB` source files first.
- `es-419` was then derived from the completed `es-ES` draft, with regional vocabulary,
  forms of address and locally used media titles changed where appropriate.
- The General deck remains a translation of the English deck. It contains no replacement
  cards or country-specific additions; cards that did not survive translation were simply
  dropped and recorded in [`general_packs/DROPPED.md`](general_packs/DROPPED.md).
- General descriptions retain the source card's explanatory sentence and its factual or
  comic kicker wherever the English card has one.
- Automated checks confirm matching keys, placeholders and line breaks in the strict
  locale files, and unchanged IDs, points and KIDS flags on retained General cards.

## App label

Register `es-419` in the app as **Español (Latinoamérica)**. Because the locale represents
many countries rather than one, use a neutral globe symbol such as **🌎** instead of a
single national flag. Register `es-ES` as **Español (España)** with **🇪🇸**.

Native review should pay particular attention to short mobile labels, official localised
titles in the General deck, and vocabulary that varies between Latin American countries.
The titles *Un final en suspense*, *Hacer un photobomb*, *Un concurso de miradas*, *El
verano del 69* and *Netflix and chill* especially need a playability decision from a native
reviewer because everyday usage varies by age and country.
